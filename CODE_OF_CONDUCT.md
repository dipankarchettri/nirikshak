# Code of Conduct

Nirikshak follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas rather than people,
and assume good faith. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not acceptable in issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer,
[@dipankarchettri](https://github.com/dipankarchettri), privately using the contact details on
their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close
threads, or block participants who break this code.
